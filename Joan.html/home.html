Founder and CEO

Atimango Joan is the founder of BE HER, a youth empowering initiative focused on leadership, mentorship, and career growth of women. She has always believed that when women are equipped with confidence, education, and opportunity, entire communities change.

What BE HER Stands For

BE HER is a youth-centered leadership initiative committed to developing confident, skilled, and purpose-driven women. Through mentorship, leadership training, and career guidance, BE HER nurtures potential and transforms ambition into impact.

We are a community committed to creating a safe space where young women discover their voice, build confidence, and develop the skills needed to lead meaningful lives. At BE HER, we nurture potential through mentorship, education, and community support, ensuring every woman is inspired to rise above limitations and become the best version of herself.

What We Offer

Mentorship Program

Guidance from experienced professionals for personal and career growth.

Career Empowerment

Equipping young women with skills for employment and entrepreneurship.

Leadership Development

Training programs that build confidence, vision, and influence.
